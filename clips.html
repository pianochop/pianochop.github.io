Questions

Can you fix bad audio?

Yes. Hum, echo, background noise, uneven levels and clipping are all fixable. Add repair to any plan for a small flat fee, quoted before I start.

Do I approve clips before they post?

Only if you want to. Some clients approve a weekly batch. Others let it run and review the monthly summary.

What do you need from me?

Your YouTube channel link, plus posting access to your short-form accounts through each platform's own connection, so you never share passwords. No files to send, no uploads.

Which shows does this suit?

Interview and conversation-driven podcasts, commentary shows, and educators with long-form video.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Retainers renew monthly and you can stop before the next month starts.
