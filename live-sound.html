How booking works

  1. Send the details

    Date, place, how many people and what you need amplified.

  2. Get a quote

    You get a total and what is included, usually within a day.

  3. Confirm with a deposit

    A deposit holds your date. The rest is due at the event.

  4. I show up early

    I set up, soundcheck and run the night so you do not have to.

Questions

How far ahead should I book?

Two weeks is comfortable. Last-minute dates are sometimes possible, so ask.

What do you need from the venue?

Power, a spot for the speakers and a time to load in. Tell me about stairs or tight spaces and I will plan for them.

Do you do outdoor events?

Yes, if there is power and cover for the gear. I will tell you what I need when I quote.

What if I need to cancel?

Tell me as early as you can. Move the date at no charge with a week of notice. The deposit covers my time if it is closer than that.

Can you work with a band's own sound person?

Yes. I can supply the PA and let their engineer mix, or mix the show myself.
