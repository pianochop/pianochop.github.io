Who you'll work with

I'm Els, a musician & mix engineer in Austin. I mix and master every song myself, so the person who hears your tracks first is the person who finishes them.

How a project works

  1. Tell me about it

    Send a rough mix, a reference song and what you want it to feel like.

  2. Get a quote and a date

    You get a total and a delivery date before anything starts.

  3. Hear it and respond

    I send a mix, you give notes, and I revise it.

  4. Final files

    You get release-ready WAVs.

Questions

What should I send for mixing?

Individual tracks exported as 24-bit WAV, all starting at the same point, plus a rough mix and any reference songs.

Can you record at my space?

Ask. It depends on the room and what we are recording.

How many revisions do I get?

Revisions are included. We agree on how many when I quote.

How long does it take?

I quote a delivery date when you book.
